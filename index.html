Account Deletion Request

App: VocabCard · Developer: Luminary

You can request the deletion of your VocabCard account and all associated data directly from this page, without needing to reinstall the app.

What will be deleted

Upon a verified deletion request, we will permanently delete the following data associated with your account:

  • Your account credentials (email address, username)
  • All flashcards, decks, and custom deck names
  • Your learning progress, mastery levels, and review history
  • Your Mistake Book, bookmarks, and personal notes
  • Any cloud-synced data stored in Firebase Firestore
Note: Account deletion is permanent. Once processed, your data cannot be recovered. If you only wish to stop using the app, you can simply sign out or uninstall VocabCard without requesting deletion.

How to request deletion

Send an email to support@vocabcard.example.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request – VocabCard". Please send the request from the email address associated with your VocabCard account.

✉️ Request Account Deletion

We will process your request within 7 business days and send a confirmation once the deletion is complete.

Alternative: In-App Deletion

If you still have VocabCard installed, you can delete your account immediately from within the app:

Me → Settings → Account → Delete Account

If you have any questions, contact us at support@vocabcard.example.com.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

Welcome to "VocabCard" (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). The App is developed and operated by Luminary ("we", "us", or "our"). We take your privacy and the protection of your personal information very seriously. This Privacy Policy is designed to explain how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information, as well as your rights regarding your data.

Please read this policy carefully before using the App. By using the App, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with vocabulary learning, data synchronization, and free ad-supported services, we may collect the following types of information:

1.1 Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: When you register or log in, we collect your email address and username via Firebase Authentication. If you choose to log in with Google or Huawei, we only receive basic identifiers (such as UnionID, OpenID, nickname, and profile picture) authorized and shared by those platforms.
  • Learning Content: Documents, images, and text you import or paste, as well as the names of custom decks you create. This content is used solely to extract vocabulary and generate flashcards.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Learning Records: Your flashcard list, review progress, mastery levels, mistake book, and practice history. This data is used to implement spaced repetition algorithms and personalized practice.
  • Device and Advertising Identifiers: To display ads and prevent fraud, our advertising partners (Google AdMob) may collect your Advertising ID, IP address, device model, OS version, and coarse geographic location (country/region level only).
  • App Activity: Your interactions with ads (e.g., impressions, clicks), app crash logs, and performance data (used to improve stability).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing Core Services: Creating and managing your flashcards, syncing learning progress to the cloud (Firebase Firestore) so you can restore your data after reinstalling or switching devices.
  • Personalized Learning: Adjusting word frequency and mastery levels based on your review performance.
  • Displaying Ads: The App is provided free of charge. We display ads (App Open and Rewarded Video) to sustain operations. Ads may be personalized based on your interests, but you can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.
  • Security: Verifying account security and preventing fraud and abuse.

3. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We will only share information with third parties under the following necessary circumstances:

3.1 Third-Party Service Providers (SDKs)

The App integrates the following third-party SDKs, which have their own independent privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication / Firestore): Used for account login and cloud data synchronization. Data is encrypted during transmission and storage.
    Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display App Open and Rewarded Video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and ad interaction data to provide personalized ads and measure ad effectiveness.
    Google Advertising Privacy Policy
  • HUAWEI Account Kit: If you choose to sign in with a Huawei account, this service is provided by Huawei Device Co., Ltd.
    Huawei Privacy Statement

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may access, use, preserve, or disclose your information if we sincerely believe it is necessary to comply with legal obligations, protect user safety, or maintain our legal rights.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Storage Location: Your learning data is primarily stored locally on your device (SQLite). If you log in, data is encrypted and synchronized to Google Cloud (Firebase Firestore) servers.
  • Security Measures: We use industry-standard security measures (such as HTTPS encrypted transmission and Firebase Security Rules) to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
  • Retention Period: We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide you with our services. If you delete your account, we will synchronously delete the learning data stored in the cloud.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and Correction: You can view and modify your learning records, notes, and account information at any time within the App.
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account at any time in "Me → Settings". Once deleted, your cloud learning data will be permanently erased and cannot be recovered.
  • Ad Personalization: You can limit ad tracking or reset your Advertising ID through your device's system settings (e.g., Android's "Ads" settings or iOS's "Privacy & Security → Tracking").
  • Withdraw Consent: If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK, you can change your ad consent preferences at any time via the AdMob UMP SDK dialog.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the information below, and we will delete such data promptly.

7. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to, and maintained on, servers located outside of your state, province, country, or other governmental jurisdiction (such as Google's global data centers). We will ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and take appropriate safeguards.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material changes will be notified to you via in-app announcements or by updating this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.

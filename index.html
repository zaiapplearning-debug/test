1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the App's functionality, improve the learning experience, and display advertising. The following data types are collected either through our own code or through third-party SDKs.

1.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

The App does not require account registration and does not collect names, phone numbers, or precise home addresses. Only when you use the "Feedback" feature will your email client open to send a message; the content of that email is entirely up to you, and we use it solely to respond to your inquiry.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically (via Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used and to improve learning content. Firebase Analytics automatically collects the following information:

  • App-instance ID: an anonymous identifier tied to the App's installation instance.
  • Android Advertising ID: a system-level advertising identifier that users can reset or delete in Android settings.
  • Approximate location: country or region-level location inferred from a masked IP address; no precise GPS location is collected.
  • App interaction events: such as screen views, quiz starts and completions, and language switches, used to analyze feature usage.
  • Purchase history: if you make an in-app purchase, Firebase records the transaction event, including product ID and amount (no payment card information).
  • Device and diagnostic information: device model, OS version, App version, and crash logs, used to ensure stable operation.

1.3 Advertising-Related Information (if ads are enabled)

If a future version of the App integrates Google AdMob or a similar advertising service, the Google Mobile Ads SDK will automatically collect and share the following data for ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location)
  • User product interactions (App launches, taps, video views, etc.)
  • Diagnostic information (launch time, hang rate, energy usage)
  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID

Collection of the Advertising ID is optional: users may reset or delete their Advertising ID in Android system settings, which affects personalized advertising. For child users, we do not serve behaviorally targeted ads; ads are shown only in a contextual manner.

1.4 Locally Stored Information (not automatically uploaded)

To provide offline learning features, the App stores the following data locally on your device. This data is not automatically uploaded to our servers:

  • Learning progress, mastery levels, wrong-word records, and bookmarks
  • Sticker collections and star points
  • Language preference, sound/vibration settings, and study reminder time
  • Premium (lifetime) unlock status (a local flag)

Uninstalling the App deletes this local data. If you have enabled Android automatic backup on your device, this data may be backed up as part of the system's backup mechanism, depending on your device settings.

5.2 Deletion Mechanisms

You may request deletion of data through the following methods:
